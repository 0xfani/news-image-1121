One picture of Saturday 21 November 2026: the day's world news on a single 1024×1024 front page. A big dated masthead with a one-line dek naming the leads, "the day in UTC" timeline, the day in numbers, 13 illustrated story cards (3 leads + 10), each picture topped by a red "PLACE: WORD" banner and drawn to carry the facts (casualty counts, places, flags, figures), a source badge on every card, a world map with numbered pins (Antarctica included), 22 numbered "Also on" briefs in three columns (35 stories in all, 7 continents, 8 fields), and a green date-check box naming both what was left out as Friday's news and what is not yet (Sunday's ATP Finals final and Israel's coalition talks). Contested claims are attributed ("Iran says", "reportedly").

Leads: Mexico's capture of FBI most-wanted "El Chapo Isidro" (at least 11 killed in Sinaloa), Iran's claimed missile-and-drone strike on US bases in Bahrain (Bahrain reports a fire at an industrial site), and a ballistic missile on Zelensky's hometown Kryvyi Rih (2 killed, 60 wounded).

Stories were gathered from the RSS feeds of BBC, Guardian, NYT, Washington Post, NPR, Sky, Al Jazeera, France 24, Euronews, Anadolu, CNA, Straits Times, SCMP, The Hindu, Africanews, Premium Times and RNZ, keeping only items first published on 21 November in UTC. Stories already reported on 20 November (Russia's strike on Kyiv's bridges, the missiles in Poland, Denmark's hit-plot claim, the UK–EU deal, GTA VI's first-day sales) are left out and named in the date-check box.

The repository holds the finished page as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to the output folder. No network, no randomness: every run writes identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python main.py   # writes entry.png to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR, or /output in the make.fun sandbox
```
